# GhostWriter — Natural AI Humanizer Studio

GhostWriter is an advanced AI text humanizer designed to transform robotic, easily detected AI output into authentically human writing that bypasses AI classifiers (such as GPTZero, Turnitin, CopyLeaks, and Winston AI).

It achieves this by replacing synthetic sentence symmetry with natural human nuances: conversational shifts, authentic parenthetical tangents, extreme sentence burstiness, realistic keyboard slips, and verification powered by **TypeSafe AI's Jev (System One)** model.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### 1. Paste Your AI Text or Load a Preset
- Paste any text generated by ChatGPT, Claude, or other LLMs into the **AI-Generated Input** box on the left.
- Or click one of the **sample presets** at the top:
  - *Robotic Essay on Social Media* (Academic fluff with "delve", "tapestry", "crucial")
  - *Formulaic Corporate Email* ("I hope this email finds you well", "synergistic")
  - *Overly Polite Tech Advice* ("paradigm shift", "imperative to remember")
  - *Generic Self-Help Paragraph* ("holistic", "unwavering resilience")

### 2. Choose a Human Voice Preset
Select the tone that matches your intended audience:
- 🛋️ **Casual Bro (Discord / Voice Chat)**: Natural informal flow, relaxed contractions, and conversational shifts (`"look, honestly"`, `"wait also,"`, `"anyway,"`).
- 🎓 **Exhausted Student (Late Night)**: Sharp substance, zero template filler, high sentence length burstiness.
- ☕ **Coffee Chat**: Warm breath pauses, personal reflections, and thoughtful conversational detours.
- 💬 **Reddit Poster**: Grounded analogies, parenthetical remarks, and candid skepticism.
- 📱 **Quick DM / Text**: Snappy, skips unnecessary filler, authentic messaging cadence.
- 💼 **Pragmatic Colleague**: Direct workplace communication stripped of corporate sycophancy.

### 3. Dial in the Imperfection Levers
Customize how "messy" and human the output should be:
- **Mess It Up (Casual Typos Slider - 0% to 15%)**:
  - `0%`: Clean flow with zero typos.
  - `3%`: Subtle dropped apostrophes (`dont`, `cant`, `im`).
  - `6% (Recommended)`: Natural casual typos (`teh`, `wierd`, `definetly`, `prolly`).
  - `10% - 15%`: Messy mobile typing slips and letter transpositions.
- **Subtle Tangents & Asides**:
  - `None`: Stays strictly on topic.
  - `Subtle`: 1 brief parenthetical or casual thought.
  - `Conversational`: 2–3 spontaneous tangents, memories, or mini self-corrections (e.g. `"(which still sounds crazy to me honestly)"`).
  - `Rambly`: Stream-of-consciousness human flow.
- **Sentence Burstiness**:
  - `Balanced Flow`: Natural sentence variation.
  - `Extreme Spikes 🔥`: Mixes punchy 1- to 3-word sentences (`"Not even close."`, `"Crazy, right?"`) with winding, conversational thoughts.
- **Casual Lowercase Opener**: Starts sentences casually in lowercase (e.g. `"honestly..."`).

### 4. Humanize & Review
- Click **"Humanize & Mess It Up"** or press **`⌘ + Enter`** (Mac) / **`Ctrl + Enter`** (Windows/Linux).
- Watch the estimated AI detection risk drop (e.g. from 98% AI down to 8% Authentically Human).

### 5. Inspect Your Text Across 5 Detailed Tabs
- 📝 **Text**: The clean, final humanized text. You can edit it directly, copy it to the clipboard, or export it as `.txt`.
- 🔍 **X-Ray View**: Interactive color-coded breakdown:
  - <span style="color:#22d3ee">Cyan</span>: Natural tangents and cognitive detours.
  - <span style="color:#fbbf24">Amber</span>: Casual keyboard slips and typos.
  - <span style="color:#34d399">Emerald</span>: Informal transitions and conversational shifts.
- 📊 **Rhythm Visualizer**: Side-by-side bar chart showing the flat, uniform AI cadence vs. jagged, erratic human burst spikes.
- ⚡ **Jev Verdict**: Live evaluation from **TypeSafe AI's Jev (System One)** model:
  - **Origin**: Typed Choice (`human` vs. `ai`) with probability percentage (e.g. `98% Human`).
  - **Authenticity Rating**: Typed Score from 0 to 4 (e.g. `4: 100% authentic human with natural imperfections`).
  - **AI Markers Present**: Typed Boolean (`NO` vs. `YES`).
- 🛡️ **Anti-AI Proof**: Breakdown of all removed AI buzzwords (`delve`, `tapestry`, `crucial`, `in conclusion`) and techniques applied.

## 🎙️ Real-time Live Voice & Audio Transcription

### 1. Spoken Audio Transcription (`gemini-3.5-transcribe`)
- Click **"Dictate"** next to the input area to speak directly into your microphone.
- The app records audio in high-fidelity Opus/WebM and transmits it to `POST /api/transcribe-audio`.
- Powered by `gemini-3.5-transcribe` to accurately convert natural spoken words, pauses, and cadence into text.
- Automatically inserts the transcribed text into the editor for instant humanization.

### 2. Live Voice Conversation Assistant (`gemini-3.8-live`)
- Click **"Live Voice"** in the top navigation bar to launch the real-time spoken dialogue studio.
- Powered by the **Gemini Live API (`gemini-3.8-live`)**:
  - **Low-Latency Streaming**: Streams 16kHz raw PCM audio from your microphone to the backend WebSocket at `/api/live`.
  - **Natural Spoken Response**: Receives 24kHz raw PCM audio chunks played smoothly via Web Audio API.
  - **Live Visualizer & Orb**: Displays real-time speaking pulses and interruption handling.
  - **Live Transcript & 1-Click Insert**: View spoken turns and click **"Insert into Editor"** to transfer ideas directly into your writing.

## 🔐 Firebase Auth & Firestore Cloud Persistence

GhostWriter is integrated with **Firebase Authentication** and **Cloud Firestore**:
- **Google Sign-In**: Click **"Sign In"** in the header to authenticate securely using your Google account via Firebase Auth.
- **Cloud Document Sync**:
  - Automatically saves all your humanized documents to your private Firestore collection (`users/{userId}/humanizations/{id}`).
  - Syncs your generation history across all your browsers and devices.
- **Zero-Trust Security (`firestore.rules`)**:
  - Strict Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC).
  - Ensures users can only read and write their own documents (`isOwner(userId)`).
  - Validates all document schemas, string size boundaries, and data invariants directly in security rules.

## 🧠 Why Detectors Get Fooled

AI classifiers like GPTZero, Turnitin, and CopyLeaks do not understand meaning—they measure statistical patterns:
1. **Perplexity (Predictability)**: AI uses the most statistically probable transition words (`Moreover`, `Furthermore`, `It is crucial to remember`). GhostWriter replaces these with unexpected colloquial connectors and parenthetical thoughts.
2. **Burstiness (Sentence Length Variance)**: AI generates uniform sentences of 15–20 words with repetitive subject-verb structures. GhostWriter introduces radical spikes (single-word sentences next to long, relaxed clauses).
3. **Imperfection**: Machine models never make phonetic slips or drop apostrophes in contractions. GhostWriter's customizable typo injector breaks the mathematical regularity of the text.

## 💻 Technical Architecture

- **Frontend**: React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4 + Lucide Icons + Motion.
- **Backend**: Express server (`server.ts`) running Node.js with Vite dev middleware.
- **AI Core**: Google GenAI SDK (`@google/genai`) for humanization + TypeSafe AI SDK (`@typesafe-ai/sdk`) for Jev System One classification.
